Test Plan very brief

Does it work as expected? Any errors on normal run?
Are all file inputs and outputs are correct.
Test what happens when things work, when they fail and at the limits.
Make sure all the business rules and steps work as they should. Do we know all the business rules are we guessing
Check that errors are handled correctly. no wild abends
Make sure the data is correct and nothing is missing or changed. 
Check that logs and records are saved correctly in new files
Make sure the script does what it is meant to do!!
Test that any changes have not broken anything that worked before!!!!
